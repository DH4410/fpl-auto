# Fantasy Premier League (FPL) Definitive Blueprint

A comprehensive reference guide detailing FPL core mechanics, market economics, the complete point scoring matrix, and advanced chip strategies for Blank and Double Gameweeks.

---

## 1. Core Game Mechanics

### Squad Building & Team Structure
* **The Roster:** You must select exactly **15 players**.
* **The Budget:** Your total squad value cannot exceed **£100.0m** at the start of the season.
* **The 3-Player Limit:** You can choose a maximum of **3 players** from any single Premier League club.

### Lineup Rules
* **Active 11:** Only the 11 players in your starting lineup score points.
* **Formations:** You can play any formation, provided you have at least **1 Goalkeeper**, **3 Defenders**, and **1 Forward** active.
* **The Bench:** Players are ranked 1–3 on your bench. If a starting player plays 0 minutes, the first eligible bench player automatically steps in based on your ranking.

---

## 2. The Transfer Market & Economy

### Transfer Rules
* **Free Transfers:** You get **1 free transfer** every Gameweek.
* **Banking Transfers:** You can save up and bank a maximum of **5 free transfers** at a time.
* **Points Penalties:** Every extra transfer you make beyond your free allowance costs **-4 points** (known as taking a "hit").

### Price Changes
* Player prices fluctuate dynamically based on how heavily the FPL community is buying or selling them. 
* **Selling Profit Tax:** If a player's value rises while in your team, you only keep **50% of the profit** (rounded down to the nearest £0.1m) when selling them. For example, if you buy a player at £6.0m and his price rises to £6.2m, you can sell him for £6.1m.

### Work-Rate Points (Defensive Contributions)
Players receive an automatic **+2 points** for hitting specific match work-rates:
* **Defenders:** Achieve 10 combined Clearances, Blocks, Interceptions, and Tackles (CBIT).
* **Midfielders/Forwards:** Achieve 12 combined CBIT actions plus Ball Recoveries.

### Bonus Points System (BPS) Mechanics
The top 3 performing players in a match receive **3, 2, and 1 bonus points**. 
* **No Dribble Punishment:** Players are **not penalized (-1 BPS) when tackled**, upgrading attacking wingers and creative full-backs.
* **No Overlap:** BPS logic prevents players from double-dipping by earning both Defensive Contribution points and high BPS simultaneously, giving pure attackers a better edge at winning bonus points.

## 4. Mastering the 8 Chips

FPL grants you **two identical sets of 4 chips** (8 total) across the season. Set 1 expires at the Gameweek 19 deadline. Set 2 launches in Gameweek 20 and lasts until the end of the season. You can only play **one chip per Gameweek**.

1. **Wildcard:** Make unlimited, permanent transfers for free to overhaul your entire squad.
2. **Free Hit:** Make unlimited transfers for **one Gameweek only**. Your original team returns the following week.
3. **Triple Captain:** Your captain’s points are multiplied by **3x** instead of 2x.
4. **Bench Boost:** The points scored by all 4 of your bench players count toward your total score for that week.

## 5. Elite Strategy: Navigating Blanks & Doubles

Because of cup clashes, certain Premier League matches get postponed. This creates two critical strategic windows:
* **Blank Gameweek (BGW):** A week where certain teams do not play at all. Their players score **0 points**.
* **Double Gameweek (DGW):** A week where certain teams play **two matches** within the same Gameweek. Their scores are combined into one massive Gameweek total.

### Strategy 1: The "Dead-Ending" Strategy (For Blank Gameweeks)
When a major Blank Gameweek approaches, do not use transfers to buy players who will skip matches. 
* **The Play:** "Dead-end" your team. Use your free transfers weeks in advance to build a starting 11 filled strictly with players who *do* have a fixture in the Blank Gameweek.
* **The Payoff:** This allows you to survive the Blank Gameweek with a full squad of playing athletes *without* wasting your Free Hit chip. Immediately after the blank week passes, trigger your **Wildcard** to instantly fix your team and dump the budget players you bought to get through the blank.

### Strategy 2: The 3-Week Chip Combo (The Ultimate DGW/BGW Strategy)
The most explosive point-scoring strategy in FPL relies on executing a precise three-chip sequence across a consecutive three-week window when a Double Gameweek is immediately followed by a Blank Gameweek:

* **Week 1 (The Setup) — Play Wildcard:** Activate your Wildcard right before a massive Double Gameweek. Use your unlimited transfers to load your squad with 15 players who all play twice in the upcoming double week. 
* **Week 2 (The Explosion) — Play Bench Boost:** Activate your Bench Boost during the Double Gameweek. Because you prepared your squad the week before, you will have **15 active players playing 2 games each** (essentially 30 fixtures scoring points for your team in a single week).
* **Week 3 (The Escape) — Play Free Hit:** The teams that played twice in Week 2 will often be the exact same teams that sit out and blank in Week 3. Activate your Free Hit to field a temporary, elite 11-man squad of players who have fixtures, completely dodging the 0-point blanks. The following week, your powerhouse double-week roster automatically returns.

### Strategy 3: The Triple Captain Rule of Thumb
* **Never** use your Triple Captain chip on a player playing a single game, no matter how good the fixture is.
* Save it for a Double Gameweek featuring an elite talisman who has two highly favorable fixtures. A secure premium asset playing 180 minutes of football provides the highest statistical ceiling for a 3x multiplier.
